Week Three

Charts 6 & 7: Should Congress raise the federal minimum wage?

The US federal minimum wage has been eroded by inflation. Despite several increases, the current minimum wage of $7.25/hr is roughly half its 1968 value.

More than half of states have increased their minimum above the federal floor. Federal minimum wage should be increased to $15/hr to match 1968 value.

Sources: U.S. Department of Labor, Federal Minimum Wage Rate (STTMINWGFG), via FRED; U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, CPI for All Urban Consumers (CPIAUCSL), via FRED; NCSL, State Minimum Wages (rates as of July 1, 2026). Real wages in 2025 dollars and state median calculated by author.
